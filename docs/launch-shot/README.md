# Launch shot

Linear [NOM-18](https://linear.app/brth31/issue/NOM-18). A launch is real,
necessary, and **one-shot**. Nothing in this directory is a post. Do not
submit to Hacker News, Product Hunt, or a subreddit from a PR.

## Hard gates — all three, no exceptions

- [ ] **W2 shipped** — observe mode, console, and policy generation are on
      `main` (NOM-11 / children). *This gate is met as of 2026-08-14.*
- [ ] **W3 delivered** — six placements **live** with inbound URLs in
      [docs/placements/README.md](../placements/README.md). *Not met.
      Every row is still draft.*
- [ ] **W5 case studies** — three published studies with partner numbers
      and written sign-off. *Not met. Public page is empty on purpose.*

If a gate is unchecked, the launch waits. Missing the gates to hit a date
is the most expensive mistake in the project.

## Day-of discipline

Be present for the whole day. Answering every comment personally matters
more than the post. Volunteer the in-process wrapping caveat **before**
someone else finds it.

After posting (human only): measure traffic and activation against the W1
baseline, and track the launch cohort’s retention separately
([docs/retention-gate.md](../retention-gate.md)). A spike that does not
retain is information, not success.
